## **Siddhpur Matrugaya – Religious Significance & Services**

* **Sanatan Dharma tradition** states that the sacred places established in different eras of Bharatvarsha (India) include those where the *Pitru Karma* (rites for ancestors) must be performed in the present age. These include **Gaya (Bihar)** and **Nabhi Gaya (Siddhpur, Gujarat)**, with Gaya associated with Lord Vishnu and Nabhi Gaya with Lord Brahma.

* In India, there are **five major sacred pilgrimage lakes (Tirth Sarovars)**:

  * **Man Sarovar** in the Himalayas
  * **Pushkar Sarovar** in Rajasthan
  * **Bindu Sarovar** at Siddhpur, Gujarat
  * **Narayan Sarovar** in Kutch, Gujarat
  * **Pampa Sarovar** near Hampi in South India

* **Devaahuti Mata** was married to **Rishi Kardam**, and this marriage is considered the **first marriage of creation**. From Devaahuti Mata’s womb, **nine divine daughters** were born, who were married to great sages. From these sages came the principal lineages of humanity:

  * Atri, Kashyap, Bharadvaj, Bhargav, Manu, Vashishtha, Gautam, Aangiras, and others.

* Devaahuti Mata performed intense penance for many years. As a result, **Lord Vishnu incarnated as Lord Kapil**, the **first incarnation to appear on Earth in human form**, at **Bindu Sarovar**.

  * Lord Kapil imparted the **first knowledge of Sankhya Yoga** during his childhood.

  * Through this divine knowledge, Devaahuti Mata attained **liberation (moksha)** from the cycle of birth and death.

  > *Teaching:* Freedom from repeated birth and death is attained through true knowledge.

* Lord Kapil gave a **divine boon** that:

  > Any person—son or daughter—who performs **Pind Daan** at this **Siddha Bhoomi (Siddhpur)** will help liberate their mother’s soul.

* After this, **Lord Kapil Narayan** went on pilgrimage across Bharatvarsha and later performed intense penance for **thousands of years** at **Gangasagar Tirth** (near present-day Kolkata), where the Ganga meets the ocean. On **14 January**, he attained divine realization and came to be known as **“Siddhanam Kapilo Muni.”**

* The greatness of this sacred land is described in:

  * **Shrimad Bhagavat Purana (Third Skandha)**
  * One of the **18 major Puranas (Garuda Purana)**

* Since **2007**, the **Government of Gujarat**, through the **Gujarat Pavitra Yatradham Vikas Board**, has been actively involved in the **restoration and development of the Siddhpur Matrugaya Tirth**.

* Even today, **saints, sages, national leaders, spiritual heads, and renowned artists** perform **Matrupind Daan** here and experience profound spiritual peace and liberation. Notable figures include:

  * Jagadguru Shankaracharya
  * Dongre Maharaj
  * Narsinh Mehta
  * Rameshbhai Oza
  * Sadhvi Uma Bharati
  * Mahatma Gandhi (Father of the Nation)
  * Former Prime Minister Devi Gowda
  * Former President Pratibha Patil
  * Ambani Family – Mukesh Ambani Group
  * Amitabh Bachchan
  * Anuradha Paudwal

* Inspired by the teachings of **Adi Shankaracharya**, followers from **all sects of Sanatan Dharma**, from every corner of **India and abroad**, come here in large numbers to perform **Matrupind Daan rituals**.
